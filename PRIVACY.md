# One Focus — Privacy Policy

One Focus is a personal desktop app. It has no servers and collects no data.

- **What it accesses:** the tasks in one Google Tasks list you choose (default "One Focus"), so it can show your current task and let you add, edit, complete and delete tasks in that list.
- **Where data goes:** only between your computer and Google. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party.
- **What it stores:** your settings, your time log, and an encrypted Google sign-in token, all in a local file on your computer.
- **Removing access:** click Disconnect in One Focus Settings, or remove access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder removes everything it stored.

One Focus's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
